# What authority turns observed behavior into a complete program?

This is a written, unexecuted analysis proposed after the [exact supplied-machine compiler](../exact-behavioral-compiler.md). Full research and storage review 93 must precede implementation and may supersede this cut. No native model, scientific array, oracle call or GPU assay is requested.

The compiler's global result uses a complete supplied finite graph. Its finite query and serving transcript is weaker evidence. The next analysis should separate those two authorities using the frozen transcript, then map the distinction onto the actual raw-prompt generator interface.

Let f be the supplied machine's penultimate-bit function. Every recorded compiler query has length at most five, and every admitted serving word has length at most 32. Consider the finite-state alternative

\[
g(w)=\begin{cases}1,&w=a^{33},\\f(w),&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
\]

It agrees with every recorded word/output answer and differs at the unqueried word a³³, where f is zero. A finite recognizer for that one word, combined with the supplied machine, realizes g. This is a mathematical witness proposal, without an executed second organism or query. It leaves the original complete-graph certificate intact: g is a different source, deliberately indistinguishable under the weaker observed interface.

The deliverable is one small transcript analysis that authenticates all recorded words, exposes the missing authority and names the exact assumption required to exclude such alternatives. Candidates include a justified hypothesis-class/rank/state bound with an adequate distinguishing test, exhaustive coverage of a declared finite domain, a qualified equivalence oracle, or a source-derived symbolic proof that initialization, transitions and readout commute. Merely enlarging a sampled panel supplies further observations, without global certification over an unrestricted class.

Map that requirement to the native generator's real input/framing/tokenizer/truncation/EOS/seed/numerical contract. A bounded finite-vocabulary token interface has finitely many effective token sequences under fixed framing, masks and controls. Token truncation alone does not bound the raw-string domain; the separate 1–1000-character input cap supplies an additional raw-input restriction. Neither makes exhaustive coverage economical. Learned source code supplies a full computation; turning that into a source-derived symbolic transition/readout proof remains the missing compiler task. Do not insert a semantic parser, neural student or procedural pixel renderer as its answer.

Preserve the present valid certificate, every historical result and all counterexamples. Label empirical agreement, structural assumption, complete proof and serving cost separately. This should settle the compiler's inference contract, without becoming a toy-family expansion or a native-image Hankel campaign. Arbitrary-prompt zero-neural image generation remains active and unsolved.
