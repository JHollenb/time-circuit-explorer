\[
U_{j:}Z_{i:}^T=U_{j:}\mu_s^T+
U_{j:}(Z_{i:}-\mu_s)^T.
\]

The candidate keeps all channel anchor contributions and only selected centered contributions:

\[
\widehat P_{ij}=a_{ij}\left[
U_{j:}\mu_s^T+
\mathbf 1_{j\in S}U_{j:}(Z_{i:}-\mu_s)^T

\]

Thus the ideal missing write is

\[
(O_{\rm centered})_{i:}=\sum_{j\notin S}
a_{ij}\left\langle U_{j:},Z_{i:}-\mu_s\right\rangle D_{:j}^T.
